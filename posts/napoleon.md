### Phase 1: The Ascent

1. **Siege of Toulon (1793)**
   The origin story where the 24-year-old artillery captain took Fort l'Éguillette and forced the British fleet to evacuate.
   * Link: [Wikipedia - Siege of Toulon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siege_of_Toulon)

2. **13 Vendémiaire (1795)**
   The street battle in Paris where Napoleon cleared Royalist rebels with "a whiff of grapeshot," saving the Revolutionary government.
   * Link: [Wikipedia - 13 Vendémiaire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/13_Vend%C3%A9miaire)

3. **Battle of Arcole (1796)**
   The charge across the bridge under Austrian fire that created the myth of his physical invulnerability.
   * Link: [Wikipedia - Battle of Arcole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Arcole)

4. **Battle of the Pyramids (1798)**
   The romantic Egyptian expedition where French infantry squares crushed the Mamluk cavalry.
   * Link: [Wikipedia - Battle of the Pyramids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_the_Pyramids)

   After this The Coup of 18 Brumaire takes place and Napoleon becomes First Consul of the French Republic.

5. **Battle of Marengo (1800)**
   The narrow, dramatic victory secured by General Desaix that cemented Napoleon's hold on political power as First Consul.
   * Link: [Wikipedia - Battle of Marengo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Marengo)

This phase represents the arrival of Napoleon, how his raw talents with ongoing chaos in this new meritocratic society thrusted him upwards. Between 1800 and 1804 he wrote "Napoleonic Code", established the Bank of France also stabilized the currency, built the Légion d'honneur (honoring achievement, not noble blood), and signed the Concordat of 1801, healing the violent rift between France and the Catholic Church.

Napoleon delivered those words before the Council of State (Conseil d'État) on May 8, 1802 (18 Floréal An X),
« On a tout détruit, il s'agit de recréer. Il y a un gouvernement, des pouvoirs, mais tout le reste de la nation, qu'est-ce ? Des grains de sable.
Nous sommes épars, sans système, sans réunion, sans contact. Tant que j'y serai, je réponds bien de la République, mais il faut prévoir l'avenir. Croyez-vous que la République soit définitivement acquise ? Vous vous tromperiez fort. Nous sommes maîtres de la faire, mais nous ne l'avons pas, et nous ne l'aurons pas, si nous ne jetons pas sur le sol de France quelques masses de granit. »

English Translation:
"We have destroyed everything; now we must rebuild. We have a government, we have authority, but what is the rest of the nation? Grains of sand.
We are scattered, without a system, without union, without contact. As long as I am here, I will answer for the Republic, but we must foresee the future. Do you believe that the Republic is definitively established? You would be sorely mistaken. We have the power to create it, but we do not possess it yet, and we never will, unless we cast upon the soil of France a few masses of granite."

### Phase 2: The Apex

6. **Ulm Campaign (1805)**
   The masterclass of strategic encirclement where an entire Austrian army surrendered without a major frontal pitched battle.
   * Link: [Wikipedia - Ulm Campaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ulm_campaign)

7. **Battle of Austerlitz (1805)**
   The "Battle of the Three Emperors"—Napoleon's tactical masterpiece that dismantled the Holy Roman Empire.
   * Link: [Wikipedia - Battle of Austerlitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Austerlitz)

8. **Battle of Jena–Auerstedt (1806)**
   The twin battles that completely broke the Kingdom of Prussia and opened the gates of Berlin to French troops.
   * Link: [Wikipedia - Battle of Jena–Auerstedt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Jena%E2%80%93Auerstedt)

9. **Battle of Eylau (1807)**
   The horrific winter bloodbath against Russia in a blizzard, marked by Murat's historic 11,000-man cavalry charge.
   * Link: [Wikipedia - Battle of Eylau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Eylau)

10. **Battle of Friedland (1807)**
    The decisive victory that forced Tsar Alexander I to the negotiating table at Tilsit, marking the absolute zenith of Napoleon's European empire.
    * Link: [Wikipedia - Battle of Friedland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Friedland)

### Phase 4: The Collapse & The End

13. **Battle of Borodino (1812)**
    The apocalyptic single-day meat grinder outside Moscow that resulted in over 70,000 casualties and preceded the burning of the city.
    * Link: [Wikipedia - Battle of Borodino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Borodino)

14. **Battle of Leipzig / Battle of the Nations (1813)**
    The largest battle in Europe prior to World War I, where the Coalition surrounded and shattered the French Empire in Germany, leading to Napoleon's first exile to Elba.
    * Link: [Wikipedia - Battle of Leipzig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Leipzig)
